The knowledge was never lost. The doses were.

Ethiopia has been documenting its own medicinal plants for generations — 1,054 herbs and 573 multi-herb prescriptions in the public database, and roughly 80% of the country still relies on it for primary care. What was missing was a scale. So we built one.

The problem

"Seven young leaves." "A handful." "Two centimetres of root."

Read a dozen ethnobotanical studies and you will find that Ethiopian dosages are counted in leaves, handfuls, spoonfuls and finger-lengths — and that children get a quarter of an adult's coffee cup. Practices that old and that good deserve a measurement. They just never had one, because nobody was measuring what was going into the pot.

The observation

We are not inventing this

Two thirds of all plant medicines were handed down inside families, mostly from father to first-born son. The knowledge is intact. Only the dosing is informal.

The name

Medhane — መዳሓን

Amharic for salvation, deliverance, recovery. The bitter leaf on the coffee saucer and the bitter drink at the end of a meal were never the point — the coming back the next morning was. MD for the mark, መዳሓን for the promise.

How a pouch is made

Four steps, and none of them is marketing

Buy the part

Leaves make up about 33% of recorded preparations and roots about 30%. We buy those parts in that ratio, from 214 smallholder farms and the forest-edge gatherers who work with them.

Fix the ratio

The honey, the barley, the cress seed — the pairings were already written down in the literature. We keep the ratio and lose the improvisation.

Test the batch

Heavy metals, microbiology and marker content, every batch. Dried moringa sold loosely has been found at 352 mg/L lead in the literature — that is exactly why this step exists.

Print the caution

On the lid, not on a web page. Four of our nine blends are unsuitable in pregnancy and every one of them says so in the same place as the price.

Quality

If we can't test it, we don't sell it.

Dried moringa leaf powder sold loose in Ethiopian markets has been found at 352.0 mg/L lead in the literature, with mineral content varying by soil, fertiliser and storage. Ethiopian-grown Moringa is genuinely excellent — 2016 to 2620 mg calcium and 1817 to 1845 mg potassium per 100 g — and it is also exactly the kind of powder that skips testing. So we do not skip it.

  • Metals
    Lead, arsenic, cadmium, mercury on every lot
  • Micro
    Total plate count, Salmonella, E. coli, yeast and mould
  • Marker
    Active content against the published range
  • Record
    Result printed on the lid, batch number kept for 3 years
The last thing we'll say

None of this is a substitute for a clinic.

If you have dysentery, malaria, a persistent ulcer, a pregnancy or a medication list, see a clinician. We sell traditional remedies as foods and dietary supplements because they are a real and well-documented part of how this country stays well — not because they replace anyone.
