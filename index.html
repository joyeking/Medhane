Teff & Shiferaw

Teff is 12.8–20.99% protein, 8% fibre, high in iron, calcium, zinc and magnesium, with a glycemic index of 57–74 and no gluten. Moringa leaf adds 28–29% crude protein and 826–1530 mg calcium per 100 g. Stirred, it is injera in a cup.

For

2 scoops in water, shakshuka or kolo
Key

ጨጋታ — bitters

Gesho & Besso

Gesho is the Ethiopian bittering plant for tej and tella — 70.5% carbohydrate, 25.6% fibre, 8.5% protein. Leaves are used for stomachache, diarrhoea, fever and loss of appetite. Besso, roasted barley powder, is the recorded antidote taken alongside strong preparations.

For
Appetite, heaviness after meals, bloating
How
Half tsp in warm water, 30 min before food
Key
Gesho leaf & stem, roasted barley
Taste
Sharply bitter, roasted — meant to be bitter
Full details
ጉርጋ — for the body

Shiferaw & Tikur Azmud

Moringa leaf powder has been screened in Ethiopia as a local nutritional supplement — it prevented the rise in cholesterol, triglycerides and LDL induced by a high-fat diet. Tikur azmud (Nigella sativa) is one of the most widely phytochemically studied plants in the country.

How it's made

Same four steps your grandmother used. Just with a scale.

Sourced

Direct from 214 smallholder farms and the forest-edge gatherers who work with them. Harvest windows are recorded, not guessed.

Matched

Leaf and root are the parts used most in Ethiopian practice — leaves 33%, roots 30%. We buy those parts, in that ratio.

Tested

Every batch goes out for heavy metals, microbiology and marker content. The result is printed on the lid, not hidden on a web page.

Dosed

No handfuls. One scoop equals one day of use, because the variable spoonful is exactly why the old way drifted.
